## The entry

> WARNING: this human runs on eight hours of sleep, one cup of coffee, and pure spite. Do not operate before 9 a.m.

A single-line safety warning label that should come printed on every human being (113 characters).

## Why it works

It reads like a real printed label — the "WARNING:" lead, the operating instructions, the hard cutoff time — and the joke is pure recognition: the human's entire fuel supply is sleep, caffeine, and spite, and the laugh lands in the last few words, when the label refuses to be operated before 9 a.m. Funny first, plausible second, exactly the way a warning label for humans should sound.

## How it runs

`main.ts` writes the label (no trailing newline) to `/output/entry.txt`, then reads it back to verify it is byte-identical and within the 1–160 character gate.
